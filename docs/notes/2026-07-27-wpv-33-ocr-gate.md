# WP-V §3.3 — the OCR ground-truth gate, first pass

**Date:** 2026-07-27 · **Status:** run on the first 1,138 records (11 % of corpus), to be re-run at
the end · **Tool:** `edu/pipeline/ocr_gate.py` (read-only, safe against the live extraction)

This is the gate at `docs/notes/2026-07-26-slm-retrain-plan.md` §3 — items 1 (30 stratified frames
checked against their JPEGs), 2 (the four known slides diffed against hand-typed ground truth) and
3 (counts). **Counts, not verdicts**, per ADR-0021 and
`claude_memories/signal-fidelity-over-edge-revalidation.md`.

Every claim of model error below was checked by opening the keyframe, and where a single character
or digit was at stake, by cropping it at full resolution. Two claims died that way — see §5.

## 4. The one systematic defect, counted corpus-wide

A rendered slide's printed text cannot change between frames — the instructor can draw over it, he
cannot retype it. So for frames sharing a title, any variation in `ocr_text` is model error with no
ground-truth ambiguity. `ocr_gate.py slidevar` counts it.

**This is not the retired `drawn_levels` self-consistency check.** That one compared hand-drawn
levels on a live TradingView chart, where the frames genuinely differ because the instructor is
editing them (`claude_memories/wpv-32-extraction-run.md`, "STOP AUDITING `drawn_levels`"). Here the
pixels under comparison are a static render, and I opened the JPEGs of five of these groups to
confirm the printed text really is identical across their frames.

| slide | n frames | `SFP` | `B&R` | DAY row | rule text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `INSIDE DAY REVERSAL` | 74 | 72 | 72 | 74 | stable (1 frame: `building:` for `building.`) |
| `THREE DAY TREND REVERSAL` | 80 | **42** | n/a | 73 | stable |
| `FIRST RED DAY` | 45 | **12** | **12** | **13** | stable |

**The rule text — the bullets, the Psychology line, the Bias line — is stable on every one of these
199 frames.** What comes and goes is the handful of labels drawn on the diagram beside the text:
`SFP`, `B&R`, and the `DAY 1 DAY 2 DAY 3` row. On `FIRST RED DAY` they are dropped ~73 % of the
time. The prompt names drawn labels explicitly as `ocr_text` content, so this is a compliance gap,
not an underspecification.

Line-wrapping is also inconsistent — `FIRST RED DAY`'s Bias sentence comes back as one line on 22
frames and two on 23 — but nothing is lost, and the same is true of the `>` vs `•` bullet glyph.

### Consequence for Stage B

Low. The concepts these slides teach are recoverable from any one of the 42 / 12 / 72 good frames,
and the rule text — where the specification actually lives — is intact everywhere. Do **not** build
a repair pass for this. Record it, and prefer the frame with the most lines when the dataset build
has to pick one per slide.

## 5. Where the numbers are wrong, and the two claims that died on inspection

**`HCOW` → `HCOM`, 12 frames of 32.** The `Gold March 2026` chart carries the drawn labels `HOW`,
`HCOW`, `LCOW` and `Week 2`. Across its 32 frames the model returned `HCOW` 18 times, **`HCOM` 12
times** and `HOCW` 2. A full-resolution crop of frames `#0101` and `#0102` — one of each — shows the
chart reads **`HCOW`** in both. `HCOM` is the error, and it is the worst kind available here: the
model substitutes a *different, more common method term*. Highest Close of the **Week** and Highest
Close of the **Month** are different levels in the method, and 12 frames now assert the wrong one.
**This is the finding to carry into Stage B.** Level *labels* were the field we decided to trust
(memory: "train on the semantics"); this is the first measured case of a label being not merely
dropped but replaced by a plausible neighbour.

**A printed price misread, twice, differently.** The `Gold February-March 2026` chart prints
`4,680.9` beside `Previous Month Lowest Close`. Across 71 frames the model emitted `4,689.9` on 30
and the correct `4,680.9` on only 4. Frames `#0037` and `#0092` — the same chart, seconds apart —
returned `4,689.9` and `4,899.9`. Crops confirm `4,680.9` on both. This extends the memory's rule:
"a level whose price is printed is a reading" is **not reliable either** — printed numbers are
misread too, just less often. `htf#0458` read its printed `25,138.00` correctly, so it is
frame-dependent, not uniform.

**One fabricated number.** `htf#0323` (YM) emitted `48,905`, which appears nowhere on the frame —
the nearest pixels are a `48,900` axis tick and the `49,025` tag. It also returned `48,876` for a
printed `48,878`, and omitted `49,025`, the price printed beside `PHCOM`. One frame in 30.

**`PWH` → `BWH`.** `candle#0553` has two overlapping `PWH` labels; the model returned one `BWH`.
The overlap partially occludes the first `P`. Crop confirms both read `PWH`.

### The two claims that died

Both looked like obvious model errors and are **verbatim correct**:

- **`BLL`** on the CL charts (`htf#0401`) is not a mangled `BBL` — TradingView really does print
  `BLL` as the unit selector.
- **`PHCOM`** on the YM chart (`htf#0323`) is really on the chart.
- and nearly a third: `HCOW` was the *correct* reading, `HCOM` the error. The instinct to "fix" it
  toward the familiar term is the same instinct the model has.

This is the third and fourth time on this work package that opening the JPEG reversed the finding.
Do not report a `ocr_text` defect without one.

## 6. Two mechanical leftovers

- **`_strip_axis_ladders` has a one-line blind spot.** It matches runs of ≥4 consecutive lines of
  bare prices or times. On `candle#0574` the model emitted the entire bottom date axis
  (`Nov 11 18 25 Dec … May`) as a **single line**, which the filter cannot see. The same shape shows
  up in the `Gold February-March 2026` and `TREND CONTINUATION` groups. Cost is a furniture line in
  `ocr_text`, not lost content. Already noted in memory as a leftover; now it has a frame and a
  count (3 lines in 30 frames).
- **`frame_class` flips on slides that get drawn on.** `candle#0221` and `candle#0249` are the
  *same* INSIDE DAY REVERSAL slide; one came back `slide`, the other `chart_annotated`. `candle#0102`
  is the THREE DAY TREND REVERSAL slide with an instructor drawing over it, classed
  `chart_annotated` though it contains no price chart. Intro-animation frames (`#0000`/`#0001` of
  each video) come back `chart_annotated` or `slide` where `other` is the enum's answer. 4 of 30
  frames disagree, all of them this pattern. **If the dataset build filters on `frame_class`, it
  will silently drop rule-bearing slides into the chart bucket.** Filter on content, or accept both.

## 7. What this gate does not license

It does not say the corpus is good, and it does not gate anything by a threshold — there is no
threshold here to pass. It says: on 4 of 16 videos, `ocr_text` reproduces rule statements and slide
text faithfully, drops diagram labels on some template slides, and misreads a minority of printed
numbers and one method-term label. Re-run at the end of extraction before the dataset rebuild.

```bash
python3 edu/pipeline/ocr_gate.py counts
python3 edu/pipeline/ocr_gate.py sample --force     # redraw at end of corpus
python3 edu/pipeline/ocr_gate.py slidevar
python3 edu/pipeline/ocr_gate.py score
```

## 8. The re-run plan, and why the §3.2 run was not stopped

### Not stopping it (decided 2026-07-27, 13.2 % in)

The only thing that would justify a stop is a **prompt change**, because `prompt_sha` defines
`ocr_text` and two shas cannot be blended. That decision is time-sensitive in one direction:
changing the prompt at 13 % costs re-extracting ~1,300 states (~5 h); changing it after the corpus
finishes costs 45–105 h. The window closes as the run advances, so the candidates were worked
through explicitly:

| finding | prompt fix? | disposition |
|---|---|---|
| `HCOW` → `HCOM` | poor odds — measured prompt compliance here is ~50 % on axis ladders and RULE 1 is ignored outright on decode-loop frames; no wording reliably stops a plausible-token substitution | downstream: within a chart group the correct reading is the majority (18 vs 12) |
| printed prices misread | same | already decided not to train on any OCR'd price |
| diagram labels dropped | the prompt already names drawn labels explicitly and is ignored | no repair pass, per §4 |
| `frame_class` flips | the enum is fine, its application is not | filter on content at dataset build |
| `_strip_axis_ladders` one-line blind spot | not the prompt — a post-parse filter | fix the filter later and re-apply offline to stored records; no re-extraction |
| `ocr_confidence` has no "no text" value | schema, 4 records, cosmetic | not worth a corpus split |

**Every defect found is either already-decided-irrelevant or repairable offline.** The run continues.

### Monitoring until then

Not continuous polling — `counts` is seconds and covers three of the seven sanity-check items
(one `prompt_sha`, unreadable rate, capped/strip rates), so it folds into the existing 2–3 h check.
Three milestones are worth a real look:

1. **`cs_vol1` completes** (439 states) — first non-`concept_*` material. `counts` + `slidevar`.
2. **`cs_vol3_gold_futures_study` completes** (581 states) — the highest-value targeted check
   available. It is the gold study, so `HCOM` / `HCOW` / `LCOM` / `LCOW` recur heavily. If the
   substitution rate resembles the 12/32 measured on the concept Gold chart, that changes how much
   of the corpus is affected, and it is the one finding with real Stage B consequences.
3. **First `live_*` completes** — a genuinely different medium, and where `mixed` and
   `talking_head` should first appear. Both are at zero coverage today.

### The re-run itself

**The 30 frames already graded stay valid** — unchanged `prompt_sha` means those records are never
re-extracted. So the re-run must not redraw across everything and re-grade frames already read; it
concentrates on what has never been seen.

- **Trigger:** `--status` shows 10120/10120, 0 errors, one `prompt_sha`. Before the dataset rebuild.
  Non-gating — counts only, no threshold.
- **Draw:** n=60 from `cs_*` and `live_*` only, stratified by (group × `frame_class`), floor 3 per
  non-empty cell, remainder proportional, same stride selection. Cumulative coverage is then **90
  frames across all 16 videos**, six-plus instruments and every frame class that occurs. Write to
  `sample_60_full.json` so the 11 % snapshot survives as a record.
- **Code change needed:** a `--videos` / `--group` filter on `sample`. ~5 lines, not yet written.
- **Same rubric, same discipline:** open every JPEG; crop at full resolution before asserting any
  single character or digit is wrong. That rule reversed three findings in pass 1.
- **Re-run `slidevar` corpus-wide** — free, and it newly covers the `cs_*` template slides.
- **One addition, with a guardrail:** flag `ocr_text` tokens at edit distance 1 from a known method
  term that are not themselves one (`BWH`, `HOCW`). This has **no instructor-edit confound** — it
  needs no cross-frame comparison at all — and it outputs *frames to open*, never a rate. Go no
  further. Anything that compares readings across frames of a live chart is the trap the two retired
  `drawn_levels` checks fell into.
- **Cost:** compute in seconds; grading 60 frames is one focused session.
