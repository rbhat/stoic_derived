| 7.4.4 | `derived` (points at §5.4.1, §5.4.4, §7.3) | See sub-checks below. | YES | See Note. |

**7.4.4 sub-checks (as instructed, checked hardest).** The rule claims *"Three of 7.4.2's instances are already mechanical under this rulebook: no clean invalidation → no PTB stop (§5.4.1); no realistic target → the room condition (§5.4.4, open); no trap side → §7.3."*

1. **"no clean invalidation → no PTB stop (§5.4.1)."** §5.4.1 reads: *"Stop = the PTB low (long) / PTB high (short)"*, Status **M** (mechanical, unconditional formula). Every signal that reaches entry has a numeric stop by construction, so this linkage holds on its own terms — no overclaim here.
2. **"no realistic target → the room condition (§5.4.4, open)."** §5.4.4 is the **stop**-distance floor (*"When \|entry − PTB extreme\| < k × ATR(n)… the stop moves behind the last swing extreme"*), Status **P**, not a target/room rule at all. The actual "sufficient room… to the Step 3 High/Low" language lives in **§5.4.6**, not §5.4.4, and §5.4.6 is itself Status **P**, expressly unresolved (references open rows **O-5**, **O-10**). §7.4.4's own parenthetical — *"(§5.4.4, open)"* — concedes the target is **not** closed, which directly contradicts the sentence's lead claim that these three are *"already mechanical."* An open (Status P) rule is not mechanical by §0's own definitions (M = "no free parameter" vs. P = "needs one number the material does not give").
3. **"no trap side → §7.3."** §7.3's **Trapped side** entry is Status **J** — "the material states it as a visual read and **explicitly refuses** to quantify it" (§0's own definition of J). Citing it as evidence that "no trap side" is "already mechanical" is the direct opposite of what §7.3 itself declares about that term's status.

Two of the three linkages (2 and 3) point at rules the rulebook's own status column marks **P** and **J** respectively — not **M** — so "already mechanical under this rulebook" overclaims relative to what §5.4.4/§5.4.6 and §7.3 themselves say about their own status. Only linkage 1 is unqualified. Classified **YES**: the word "mechanical" applied to all three items goes beyond what two of the three pointed-at rules (§5.4.4/§5.4.6 Status P, §7.3 Trapped-side Status J) establish about themselves.
