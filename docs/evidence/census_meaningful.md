# Passage census — "meaningful"

**Term 1: the Step 1 break and close beyond both MAs. Term 2: the confirming close beyond the
selected boundary.**

This file enumerates every passage in the corpus that bears on the two **"meaningful"** terms
`stoic/sequence.py` injects as undecided predicates. It is evidence, not a proposal.

**What this is.** The passage set Phase 4's SLM proposes over, and the eval set for whatever it
proposes. Per `claude_memories/coverage-claims-need-enumeration.md`, the material is not "silent"
until the directory has been listed and read — so the Coverage section at the bottom lists every
file opened.

**What this is not.** No threshold, number, predicate or algorithm is proposed here, hedged or
otherwise. `docs/RULEBOOK.md` §0 makes a number for a **J** term a strategy decision requiring the
human, and `claude_memories/audit-hard-rules-not-in-material.md` is explicit that a predicate
written to fill a silence forecloses exactly the question Phase 4 exists to answer.

**Status of the two terms in the spec.** §2.1.1 is **P**, §2.1.4 is **J**, §2.3.4 is **P**.
**D-2** covers both and sets no threshold.

**Counts, not verdicts.** Where a count appears it is a count of passages. Nothing below projects a
direction or concludes from the number of instances.

### What the material determines

- The event is **a break *and* a close**, beyond **both** MAs — stated identically in `PC`, and three
  times in `M1` (`@ 08:43`, `@ 09:47`, `@ 23:15`).
- **A wick alone does not count** (`M1 @ 10:03`) — the only exclusion stated in mechanical terms.
- **A close back inside does not complete Step 1** (`M1 @ 10:10`).
- The read is **visual** (*"meaningful visual break"*, `M1 @ 09:54`) and the operative standard is
  *"should look clear on the chart"* (`M1 @ 10:24`).
- **The tie-break is to pass** (`M1 @ 10:32`). Ambiguity has a defined resolution, and it is not a
  threshold.

### What it leaves open

- Any magnitude whatsoever. `M1 @ 10:18` **refuses** two specific quantifications by name — *a fixed
  percentage* and *a minimum candle size* — and offers no third.
- What *"meaningful"* adds beyond the two mechanical exclusions already stated. Nothing in the corpus
  distinguishes a close that clears both MAs by a little from one that clears them by a lot, other
  than `D-2`'s human decision (*"the stronger the surge, the better"*), which is a **preference
  ordering with no cut point**.
- Whether *"visual break"* is measured against the MA lines, the candle body, or something else.
- Whether both MAs must be cleared by the **same** close, or the condition is evaluated on the pair
  jointly. Every passage says *"beyond both"*; none addresses a straddle.

### Cited vs new

**9 cited, 9 new** (plus 4 logged occurrences of the word with a different referent). New passages
come from `M1` ×5, `DISC`, `TPA`, `PTBV`, `MS`. **No passage in either group quantifies.**
Three are marked *refuses*, all in `M1 @ 10:18`–`10:32`.

### What the material determines

- Confirmation is a **close**, and it is judged by **location** relative to the selected boundary —
  *"The location of the close determines whether it confirms"* (`M1 @ 14:32`).
- **A gap through the boundary still counts as a break** (`M1 @ 14:26`); the gap does not itself
  confirm.
- **A close back inside the consolidation does not confirm** (`M1 @ 14:40`), and the base may then
  remain pending while visually intact.
- The break and the confirmation are **separate events** — stated on `PC` and restated four times in
  `M1` (`@ 09:15`, `@ 14:02`, `@ 15:12`, `@ 23:38`).
- Waiting for confirmation **costs risk-to-reward** (`M1 @ 20:52`), so the choice to wait is a trading
  decision, not a spec clause.
- **The chart may not be rewritten to call something confirmed** (`M1 @ 15:07`).

### What it leaves open

- Any magnitude. *"barely swept"* (`M1 @ 19:35`) is the only magnitude word in the term, and §2.3.10
  already records that it sets no threshold.
- What separates a close that is *"way too close to the resistance"* (`M1 @ 20:36`) from one that
  confirms. The passage names the doubt and resolves it by **waiting**, not by measuring.
- Whether *"beyond the boundary"* is measured to the boundary line itself or with any allowance, on a
  sloping boundary in particular (`PC` draws one sloping — §2.2.7).
- Whether the *"obvious"* used of a confirming close by `TPA @ 00:40:42` and `PTBV @ 01:12:18` is the
  same judgment as *"meaningful"*, or the speaker's loose synonym. The corpus never defines either
  against the other.

## Are the two terms the same question, or different ones?

The corpus does not answer this directly. What the passages show:

**Evidence they are one question.**

1. **The same word, chosen deliberately, for both.** `PC` writes *"Meaningful break and close beyond
   both MAs"* and *"Meaningful close beyond the selected boundary"* on one slide. `M1 @ 10:18` says
   *"i use the word meaningful on purpose"* — the deliberateness is stated once, about a word that is
   already doing both jobs on the slide the speaker is reading from.
2. **`D-2` treats them as one.** The decision's scope column is *"§2.1.1, §2.3.4"* — a single human
   decision covering both, with one answer (*"Body, not wick. The stronger the surge, the better."*).
3. **The refusal is generic.** *"a fixed percentage or minimum candle size would create precision that
   the chart does not provide"* (`M1 @ 10:18`) names no step. It is a statement about what charts
   supply, which would apply to any close.

**Evidence they are different questions.**

1. **The reference object differs.** Term 1 is measured against **two moving averages** — lines that
   move every bar and that `M1 @ 11:14` calls *"visual guides not laser lines that price must obey to
   the tick"*. Term 2 is measured against **one selected boundary**, a line fixed before the break
   under the §2.2.8 no-hindsight constraint. A tolerance argument available for one is not obviously
   available for the other.
2. **Term 1 carries two mechanical exclusions Term 2 never receives.** *"a wick alone does not
   count"* and *"a quick test through the averages that closes back inside does not complete step
   one"* are stated at §2.1.2–§2.1.3 for Step 1. §2.3.10 **explicitly declines** to carry the wick
   rule across: *"The source says barely swept, which is about magnitude — it is not a wick-vs-body
   test."*
3. **Only Term 1 gets the explicit refusal.** `M1 @ 10:18`–`10:32`, including the *"argue with
   yourself … pass"* tie-break, sits in the Step 1 passage. Nothing equivalent is said at §2.3.4. The
   spec records this asymmetry: §2.1.4 is **J**, §2.3.4 is **P**.
4. **Different failure vocabularies.** Term 1's negative is *"closes back inside"* the MAs
   (`M1 @ 10:10`); Term 2's are *"closes back inside the consolidation"* (`M1 @ 14:40`) and *"barely
   swept"* (`M1 @ 19:35`). Only Term 2's has a magnitude word.
5. **Only Term 2 has a live case of proximity-driven doubt.** `M1 @ 20:36` — *"way too close to the
   resistance"* — has no counterpart for the MA break anywhere in the corpus.

**Reported, not settled.** Both readings have direct support. The **5 to 3** split above is a count of
passages, not a verdict, and the sets are not independent — points 2 and 3 on the "different" side
both rest on the same §2.1.2–§2.1.4 block. The corpus contains **no passage that compares the two
terms**, and none that says a rule for one transfers to the other. `D-2` bundling them is a human
decision recorded in §11, not a finding from the material.
