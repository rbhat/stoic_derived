10 Stoic Trader Commandments

1. Protect capital first.
Your first job is not to make money on the next candle. Your first job is to stay alive long enough for real edge to matter.


2. Trade context before candles.
A candle only matters after you understand the higher-timeframe map, the level, the trapped side, and the risk.


3. Treat cash as a position.
Being flat is not failure. Cash is how a Stoic Trader avoids paying for noise.


4. Require the setup to deserve risk.
A setup name is not enough. The trade must have context, asymmetrical risk to reward, invalidation, and a reason to exist.


5. Let patience create conviction.
Conviction does not come from forcing the first move. It comes from waiting and only trading if price follows the plan you set before entry.


6. Do not negotiate with live candles.
Decide the thesis, risk, invalidation, and management before pressure hits. Do not rewrite the plan because a candle scared or excited you.


7. Size belongs on asymmetry, not boredom.
Risk should match the quality of the opportunity, not your need for action.


8. No trade is a valid outcome.
Your analysis does not need to end with an entry. If price does not reach your level, confirm your thesis, or offer clean invalidation, no trade is the correct decision.


9. Review the process, not just the result.
A winning trade can still be bad process. A losing trade can still be clean process. Judge the process, not the outcome.


10. Earn the identity through repetition.
You do not become a Stoic Trader by knowing the words. You earn it by repeating the standard under pressure. One process, every day, every week, every month, every year.
