*Entry technique for 1-2-3 Sequence*
See diagrams step-3-livetrade.png, step-3-pullback-bearish-diagram.svg, step-3-pullback-diagram.svg
 
Step 3 confirms direction and creates the Step 3 High.
Once the Step 3 High is established, wait for price to pull back.
The Pullback Trigger Bar, or PTB, is simply the last candle in that pullback.
Place a buy stop above the PTB high. When price trades above it, the Entry triggers.
Confirmed Step 3 → Step 3 High → Pullback → PTB → Entry
The bearish version works in reverse. Once the Step 3 Low is established, wait for price to bounce. The last candle in that bounce is the PTB. Place a sell stop below its low.
First Target is Step 3 High or Low. This is the place to take partials and put stop to break even.
I took a short scalp on ES with this technique right after market open, 5min chart.
