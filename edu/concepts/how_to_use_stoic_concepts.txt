How To Use Stoic Trader Concepts

This is the foundation layer inside Skool.

The goal is not to give you more indicators, more noise, or more things to chase.

The goal is to give you the shared language behind Stoic Traders:

the Stoic Lens - the way we see trading and markets.

Higher-Timeframe context - Daily, Weekly & Monthly cycles.

Trapped Trader logic - we don't chase candles, we trade trapped traders.

PDH / PDL / PDC - previous day high, low and close.

HCOM / LCOM - Monthly Extremes, highest and lowest daily close of the month .

B&R / SFP - Two setups only. Break & Retest / Swing Failure Pattern.

SBS - Swing Breakout Sequence manipulation pattern.

Fib Geometry - measuring expansions and targets.

20+200 SMA - two simple moving averages for trade direction.
